FishOut! – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026
Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FishOut! ("FishOut!", "the game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the FishOut! mobile application.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the information necessary to operate the game, provide advertising and in-app purchase functionality, maintain reliability, and improve the user experience.

1. Information We Collect

FishOut! does not require users to create an account in order to play the game. However, certain information may be collected or processed automatically by third-party services integrated into the application.

Depending on your device, privacy choices, location, and the services used, this information may include:

  • IP address;
  • device identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers, where permitted;
  • general device and operating system information;
  • advertising data, such as information about advertisements shown or interacted with;
  • app interactions and usage information;
  • diagnostic information;
  • crash information;
  • performance information;
  • information necessary to provide advertising and measure advertising performance.

The exact information available to advertising providers may depend on your privacy and consent choices and on restrictions imposed by your device or operating system.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

FishOut! uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising.

The game includes rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are optional. A player may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward, such as Pearls.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary. The reward is provided according to the rules displayed inside the game after the applicable rewarded advertisement has been successfully completed and the reward event has been confirmed by the advertising system.

Google and its advertising partners may process information for purposes such as:

  • delivering advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • preventing fraud and abuse;
  • analytics;
  • maintaining and improving advertising services;
  • providing personalized advertising where legally permitted and where the required consent has been obtained.

Depending on your privacy choices and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available at: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent for Advertising

Where required by applicable law, FishOut! may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another applicable consent mechanism to request and manage privacy choices relating to advertising and the processing of personal data.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other jurisdictions where consent or privacy choices are required, users may be presented with options relating to the use of their personal data for advertising and related purposes.

Depending on applicable law and the user's choices, certain advertising features may be limited.

4. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Where permitted and where the necessary consent has been provided, advertising providers may use information to provide personalized advertising.

If consent for personalized advertising is not provided, advertising may still be displayed where permitted, but it may be non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted.

5. Tracking and Device Identifiers

Advertising services may use device identifiers or advertising identifiers where permitted by the operating system, applicable law, and the user's privacy choices.

On Apple platforms, access to certain advertising identifiers and tracking functionality may be subject to Apple's privacy requirements, including App Tracking Transparency where applicable.

FishOut! respects privacy permissions and choices made available through the user's device and applicable consent mechanisms.

6. In-App Purchases and Pearls

FishOut! offers optional in-app purchases, including packages of virtual in-game currency referred to as "Pearls".

Purchases on iOS are processed through Apple's App Store and Apple's In-App Purchase system. FishOut! does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

Apple may process transaction and payment information according to Apple's own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

Pearls are virtual in-game items intended solely for use within FishOut!. They are not real-world currency, do not represent a bank deposit or financial account, and cannot be exchanged for cash except where otherwise required by applicable law.

7. Purchase and Reward Problems

We make reasonable efforts to ensure that purchases and in-game rewards are delivered correctly. However, technical problems may occasionally occur, including network interruptions, service outages, interrupted transactions, or failures involving third-party services.

If you are successfully charged for a purchase but the corresponding Pearls are not delivered because of a technical problem, please contact FishOut! Support at:

fishout@email.cz

Please provide reasonable information necessary to investigate the issue, such as the approximate date of purchase, the product purchased, and relevant Apple transaction or purchase information where available.

Please do not send us your payment card number, banking credentials, Apple ID password, or other sensitive financial credentials.

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate verified delivery problems and, where technically possible and appropriate, assist with resolving issues involving purchased or earned in-game content.

8. Refunds

Payments for purchases made through the iOS version of FishOut! are processed by Apple.

Requests for refunds for purchases made through Apple's App Store are generally submitted to and evaluated by Apple in accordance with Apple's applicable refund policies, terms, and applicable law.

Because Apple processes App Store transactions and determines eligibility for App Store refunds, we cannot guarantee that a particular refund request will be approved.

If you experience a technical issue involving the delivery of purchased Pearls, you may also contact us at fishout@email.cz so that we can investigate the issue.

Important: Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or limit any mandatory consumer rights, statutory warranties, refund rights, or other rights that you may have under applicable law.

Information about requesting a refund for purchases made through Apple is available through Apple's official support services.
